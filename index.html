Barista · Psychology

I am Nyomi Perez, a psychology major at the University of California, Riverside. I expect to graduate in June 2028. I also work as a barista. This page holds those two parts of my week in one place: the degree I am earning, and the customer-facing job I am doing while I earn it.

School is where I study how people think, feel, and behave. I am interested in attention, memory, motivation, and the short conversations that change the tone of an ordinary day. A class can give me the words for those ideas. A shift gives me the practice. Someone wants the usual drink, someone changes the order, someone is in a hurry, and someone wants a minute of small talk. My job is to hear the request, make the drink as asked, and keep the bar moving without making the person at the counter feel brushed aside.

That routine has been useful. I repeat an order back so I do not guess. I keep the station organized when several tickets arrive together. I stay polite on the shifts that run long. The cafe is not a substitute for psychology coursework. It is separate work, and it belongs here because it is real time spent with the public.

What I want from the major is a clearer account of those moments. Why does one guest remember a missed detail, and another remember that someone listened? Why does a busy hour feel longer than the clock, and why does a calm explanation land better than a rushed one? Those are psychology questions I meet during ordinary shifts. June 2028 is the date I am giving myself to study them at the University of California, Riverside.

The University of California, Riverside is my school through June 2028, and psychology is the major. The education section names that school, that major, and the graduation month I am working toward. Course projects and campus roles can be added when they are part of my record. I would rather keep the line accurate than fill it with activities I cannot claim.

I am also learning how to publish work on the web. My GitHub account is github.com/npere143-dev, and my LinkedIn profile is linkedin.com/in/nyomiperez. This resume is the first public page connected to that GitHub account. It is one scrolling document: an introduction, experience, school, and a portfolio. The six portfolio pictures were made for this site, and they are all the same size. They point at barista work, psychology, a Southern California campus, a June 2028 graduation, a study desk, and time at a laptop learning how a page gets published. They are original graphics. They are not photographs of a specific shift, a classroom at Riverside, or a diploma I have already earned.

From now through June 2028 I want this page to stay current. The psychology major stays first. The barista role stays visible beside it. LinkedIn is the right link for a formal profile. GitHub is the right link for this site and for projects that grow after it. Thank you for reading.
